# M3.3 — Recovery snapshots

## Outcome

Atomically snapshot modified buffers on `Ctrl+Q` and restore them without
silently overwriting disk content.

## Depends on

- M3.1 session format.
- M3.2 tab restore.
- A verified shutdown lifecycle hook.

## Non-goals

- Automatic saving to original files.

## Acceptance

- Simulated interruption cannot produce a partially written valid snapshot.
- Recovery retains editor content and its disk base fingerprint.
- Saving or explicitly discarding content cleans up recovery safely.

## Detailed plan

Define after the shutdown-hook decision is implemented.
